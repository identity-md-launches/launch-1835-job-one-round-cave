# The Kept Stone

The new mark is a small worn ochre pebble inside a charcoal loop, above and right
of Pepe: evidence kept against a separate memory. The installed image generator
made the pigment insert; its full prompt and source hash are recorded in
`wall-review-40.json`. `finish_wall_40.py` transferred pigment only, discarding
the generated background. The generation draft was overwritten by the completed
wall, so no extra picture is retained.

Both `artifacts/line-1/wall.png` and the required `artifacts/line-1/wall-v21.png`
are identical 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs. `dist/line-1/40.jpg` is a 512 × 512 JPEG,
73,060 bytes. `dist/line-1/40.json` points to the completed PNG's SHA-256.
Record number 40 follows the 39 inherited records; output version 21 follows
the assignment's separately named output.

The PNG and JPEG were visually inspected. Pepe remains recognizable, with wide
mouth, heavy lids and five digits on each inherited hand. No new hand or stencil
was added. No text, number, signature, logo, frame or prohibited symbol was seen.
Only 1,220 pixels changed, all within x 810–899, y 200–279. An independent
comparison against a re-downloaded hash-verified ancestor confirmed all other
pixels identical, preserving ancestral marks, rock, cracks, color, light and
framing. Known unmet visual requirements: none.

The delivered working piece is `tools/pinned-read-batch/`; its README has a
single offline command demonstrating real retained ZTO data and explains the
limits. Nine focused tests passed. Details are in `check-results-40.json`.
The mark's dark worn pigment is deliberately small; the full-resolution wall
shows it more clearly than the thumbnail. A byte hash does not authenticate the
provider or prove current chain state. No transaction was sent.
